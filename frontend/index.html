Floodplain oxygen budget (educational model)

Educational; uncertain inputs

Potential demand is not river depletion. A changed radar signal does not give us flooded hectares. Compare the independent evidence first. Then test explicit assumptions about litter, water volume and the pathway to a river. The model never fits the oxygen measurements you will reveal. This experiment uses a fixed 14-day scenario from the selected radar timestamp; the legacy alignment lag/window controls above do not set its duration or onset.

Assumed hydrology; no event estimate

Observed inundation and routing are insufficient for an event scenario. These choices test mechanisms only; evidence-arm changes do not change their physical inputs.

Published parameters & where this differs from DODOC

Gibbs, Wallace & Mosley (2022), Table 1 supplies uniform sensitivity bounds. They are not calibrated 2026 probabilities. Single-box oxygen model (previous release)

Modeled; uncalibrated

Change biodegradable oxygen demand, temperature, water exchange and reaeration in a mixed water box. These assumed parameters are independent of the observed 2026 river records.

Initial DO 9 mg/L; inflow DO 9 mg/L; inflow demand 2 mg O₂-equivalent/L; decay at 20 °C 0.15/day; 14 days. Temperature multipliers 1.047 and 1.024 are illustrative.

Mass balance & scientific lineage

dO/dt = exchange × (Oin − O) + reaeration × (Osat − O) − consumption.
dL/dt = exchange × (Lin − L) − consumption.
Consumption = decay × L × O / (0.2 + O).

Established oxygen-budget concepts motivate this simplified model. DODOC and BRAT provide more specific floodplain formulations. This implementation uses a mixed box with oxygen-limited consumption; it has no site calibration.
